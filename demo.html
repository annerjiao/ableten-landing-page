Transcript

Ever feel like your group's best finds are scattered across dozens of tabs, chat threads, and screenshots? Ableten solves that. Capture what your crew shares, keep who-said-what attached, and build a shared second brain for better, evidence-based plans. With Ableten's Chrome extension, you can highlight anything you read and instantly save your insight with a note—no extra clicks, no friction.

Your highlights sync across your browser and web app, ready whenever you need them. Ableten automatically organizes your highlights into collections based on topic and content, so your research stays structured without manual sorting. No more hunting through bookmarks or scattered notes—your research organizes itself so you can quickly find the sources and ideas that matter most.

Got a plan forming? Pull context from your shared collections, brain dump the group's ideas, and generate grounded itineraries and drafts in seconds—with citations back to who suggested what. Export your work as Markdown or Word and let your second brain do the heavy lifting. Ableten helps your group plan from what you actually found.

Frequently asked questions

What is Ableten?

Ableten is a social second brain for group planning. Your crew's links, recommendations, screenshots, and notes land in one shared library—auto-organized into collections—so when it's time to plan, you start from what your group actually found.

How is Ableten different from generic AI planning tools?

Most tools start from a blank prompt. Ableten starts from sources your group already saved—links, highlights, screenshots, and notes—so plans are grounded in your crew's material, with citations back to who suggested what. Not AI guesses.

How is Ableten different from a notes app or second-brain tool?

Those are built for one person. Ableten is social from the start: shared collections, attribution for every rec, and itineraries the whole group can see and shape.

Can friends add recommendations to my plans?

Yes. Friends can save into a shared collection. Everyone on the plan can open the same itinerary and see who recommended each place, with notes from the original recs.

Can I use Ableten for group trip planning?

Yes—that's the flagship use. Collect everyone's recommendations and saved places, then compose a shareable itinerary with citations back to the original recs.

Is Ableten free?

Yes. Ableten is currently free, including the Chrome extension, unlimited saves, and shared collections. Questions? Visit our support center.

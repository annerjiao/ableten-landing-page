    How it works

    1. Find a trip that fits.

      Search a city or country and open any plan to see every day.

    2. Copy it in one click.

      Duplicate the itinerary into your own Ableten collection. The author's plan stays theirs.

    3. Make it yours.

      Swap stops, add your friends' recs, and let the composer rebuild the days around what your group wants.

    Why start from a traveler's plan

    Made by travelers who went.

    These are trips people took and enjoyed, not lists of top-10 attractions.

    Every stop has a source.

    Each place traces back to the link, rec, or note the traveler saved, so you can see why it's there.

    Day by day, already sequenced.

    Mornings, afternoons, and evenings are ordered with maps, so the routing is already done.

    A starting point, not a script.

    Copy a plan, then edit it with your crew in one shared collection.

    Free to copy.

    Most authors let anyone duplicate their plan at no cost.

    Private stays private.

    Only itineraries their authors chose to make public appear here.

    Itinerary world feed FAQ

    Who makes these itineraries?

    Ableten travelers who planned a real trip and chose to publish it.

    What happens when I copy one?

    You get a new collection you own, with the places and sources. The author isn't notified and you don't join their trip.

    Can I publish mine?

    Yes. In Ableten, open your itinerary, choose Share, then Public.
